Delete your StudyCircle account

StudyCircle · developed by Lokesh T and Mohneesh Kumar U

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open StudyCircle and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email 2401107076@ptuniv.edu.in from your college email address with the subject "StudyCircle account deletion". We delete the account within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your profile (name, email), sign-in account, block list, upvote history, leaderboard entries, reports you made and rate-limit records.

What is kept: doubts and answers you posted, so other students' threads still make sense. They are shown as "Deleted student" and are no longer linked to you.
