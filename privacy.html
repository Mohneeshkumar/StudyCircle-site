Privacy policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · StudyCircle, built by Lokesh T and Mohneesh Kumar U

StudyCircle is a student project for Puducherry Technological University. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

DataWhyShared with
Name and college email addressYour account; your name appears on doubts and answers you post (not on anonymous doubts)Supabase (authentication and database)
Doubts, answers, upvotes, accepted answers, reports and your block listThe core features of the appSupabase (database)
Subjects you followThe app checks for new doubts in those subjects about every 15 minutes and shows a notificationKept on your phone only

We do not sell data, show ads, or collect location, contacts, photos or payment details.

Anonymous doubts

When you post anonymously, other students never see your name. The server still records who posted it so reports and moderation work. Only the app's moderators can act on that record.

Where data is stored and for how long

Data is stored by Supabase in its Mumbai (ap-south-1) region, and encrypted in transit and at rest. We keep your data while your account exists. The app does not use crash reporting or analytics services.

Deleting your data

Delete your account in the app under Profile → Delete account, or follow the steps on the account deletion page. Deleting your account removes your profile, block list, upvote history, leaderboard entries and reports you made, and signs you out. Doubts and answers you posted stay so that threads still make sense, but your name is replaced with "Deleted student" and they are no longer linked to you.

Security

Only signed-in students with a verified college email can read content. Every write goes through server-side checks, with rate limits and integrity checks against modified apps.

Children

StudyCircle is for university students and is not directed at children under 13.
