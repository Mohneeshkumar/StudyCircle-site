Terms of use and community rules

Version 1 · Effective 29 October 2026

By using StudyCircle you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

Who can use StudyCircle

Students with a verified @ptuniv.edu.in email address. You are responsible for activity on your account; keep your password private.

Community rules

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate speech, threats, sexual content or personal attacks.
  • Help people learn. Do not post exam or assignment answers in ways that break your university's academic-integrity rules.
  • No spam, advertising or repeated posts.
  • Do not share other people's personal information.
  • Post only content you have the right to share.

Reporting and moderation

Use Report on any doubt or answer, and Block to hide a student's posts from you. Content reported by several students is hidden automatically until a moderator reviews it. Moderators may keep or remove content, and may suspend accounts that break these rules.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You allow other StudyCircle users to view it inside the app. Karma and leaderboards are for fun and have no monetary value.

No guarantees

Answers come from fellow students and may be wrong. StudyCircle is a student project provided "as is", without warranties, and may change or stop at any time.
